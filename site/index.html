03 / How the study works

Change two things. Keep the rest fixed.

The four conditions

A · Haiku + baseline
B · Opus + baseline
C · Haiku + redesigned
D · Opus + redesigned

Shared tasks, prompt, budgets, evidence rules and one repair allowance. The redesign combines several features; the study cannot isolate their individual effects.

The final evaluation

11 tasks × 4 conditions × 3 repetitions = 132 attempts.

Whole-task paired bootstrap: 5,000 resamples, seed 0. Report uncertainty, failed attempts, unresolved grading and settled cost. No claims of production reliability.

What the numbers mean

These are postings first captured in a dated window. They are not a census of open jobs. The counting identity is stored company plus normalized title; it may combine separate real positions and does not establish that two postings describe one vacancy.

Developer interviews are deferred. The 80% quality floor and 300-second median target are owner-chosen assumptions. Earlier development received aggregate totals involving three final tasks; that exposure is disclosed and separately audited. The original job descriptions and private review journals are not distributed.
